# Scientific Names of Familiar Animals

## Learning Objectives
- Match some familiar animals to their scientific names.
- Notice species that share the same genus.

## Wild Cats

**Lion:** *Panthera leo*. **Tiger:** *Panthera tigris*. **Leopard:** *Panthera pardus*. Lions, tigers, and leopards share the genus *Panthera* but are different species.

## Animals Near Us

**Domestic cat:** *Felis catus*. **Domestic dog:** *Canis lupus familiaris* (a commonly used subspecies name; *Canis familiaris* is also used in some classifications). **Human:** *Homo sapiens*.

## Other Examples

**African savanna elephant:** *Loxodonta africana*. **Asian elephant:** *Elephas maximus*. **Western honey bee:** *Apis mellifera*. **House mouse:** *Mus musculus*.

## Why Be Specific?

"Elephant" describes several species, so it is better to specify African savanna elephant or Asian elephant. "Honey bee" also refers to different species; *Apis mellifera* refers specifically to the western honey bee.

## Practice Tip

Focus on recognizing the naming pattern rather than memorizing dozens of names at once. Scientific names are especially useful when two animals have similar common names.

## Key Vocabulary
- **species:** A group of organisms classified together; species boundaries can be complex.
- **genus:** A group of closely related species.
- **subspecies:** A subdivision of a species, sometimes written using a third name.

## Common Misconceptions
- **Misconception:** All elephants have one scientific species name. **Correction:** Different elephant species have different names.
- **Misconception:** Lion and tiger have the same full scientific name. **Correction:** They share a genus but have different specific epithets.

## Check Your Understanding

### Question 1
What is the scientific name of a lion?

**Answer:** *Panthera leo*.
**Explanation:** The genus is *Panthera*.

### Question 2
Which two animals listed share the genus Panthera?

**Answer:** Lion and tiger (also leopard).
**Explanation:** Their names start with *Panthera*.

## Summary
- Scientific names distinguish organisms precisely.
- Shared genus names often indicate close relationships.
