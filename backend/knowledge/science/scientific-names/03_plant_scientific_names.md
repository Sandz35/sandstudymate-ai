# Scientific Names of Familiar Plants

## Learning Objectives
- Recognize scientific names for common plants.
- Understand that common food names can refer to species or varieties.

## Foods and Crops

**Mango:** *Mangifera indica*. **Rice (Asian rice):** *Oryza sativa*. **Wheat (common bread wheat):** *Triticum aestivum*. **Tomato:** *Solanum lycopersicum*. **Potato:** *Solanum tuberosum*.

## Flowering Plants

**Common sunflower:** *Helianthus annuus*. **China rose (Chinese hibiscus):** *Hibiscus rosa-sinensis*. **Sacred lotus:** *Nelumbo nucifera*.

## One Genus, Different Species

Tomato (*Solanum lycopersicum*) and potato (*Solanum tuberosum*) share the genus *Solanum*. They are not the same species, even though they are related.

## Be Careful With Common Names

Names such as "rose," "oak," and "banana" may refer to many species or hybrids. A scientific name is best given for a clearly identified species, not a broad everyday category.

## Real-Life Connection

Look at the label in a botanical garden. It may give both a familiar name and an italicized scientific name.

## Key Vocabulary
- **botanical:** Related to plants.
- **species:** A classification for a particular kind of organism.
- **hybrid:** An organism with parents from different varieties or species, depending on context.

## Common Misconceptions
- **Misconception:** All plants called roses have the same scientific species name. **Correction:** The term rose covers many species and varieties.
- **Misconception:** A genus and a species name must be written in all capitals. **Correction:** Only the genus is capitalized.

## Check Your Understanding

### Question 1
What is the scientific name of mango?

**Answer:** *Mangifera indica*.
**Explanation:** It is the two-part name for mango.

### Question 2
What genus is shared by tomato and potato?

**Answer:** *Solanum*.
**Explanation:** Both names start with *Solanum*.

## Summary
- Plant species names use the same genus-plus-epithet pattern.
- Common names may be broader than a single species.
