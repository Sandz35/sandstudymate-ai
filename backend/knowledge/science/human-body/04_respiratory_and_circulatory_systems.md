# Breathing, Blood, and the Heart

## Learning Objectives
- Explain how lungs exchange gases.
- Describe how the heart and blood transport materials.

## The Respiratory System

Air enters through the nose or mouth, passes through airways, and reaches the **lungs**. Tiny air sacs called **alveoli** allow oxygen to enter the blood and carbon dioxide to leave the blood.

## The Circulatory System

The **heart** pumps blood through **blood vessels**. Blood carries oxygen and nutrients to cells and takes some waste products away.

## Types of Blood Vessels

**Arteries** carry blood away from the heart. **Veins** carry blood toward the heart. **Capillaries** are tiny vessels where substances move between blood and tissues.

## How the Systems Work Together

In the lungs, blood picks up oxygen and releases carbon dioxide. The heart pumps oxygen-rich blood to the body. During exercise, breathing and heart rate usually increase to meet the muscles’ needs.

## Real-Life Connection

Place a hand over your chest after brisk walking: you may feel your heartbeat become faster. That is one way the circulatory system adjusts to activity.

## Key Vocabulary
- **alveoli:** Tiny air sacs in the lungs where gases are exchanged.
- **artery:** A vessel carrying blood away from the heart.
- **vein:** A vessel carrying blood toward the heart.
- **capillary:** A very small blood vessel connecting smaller arteries and veins.

## Common Misconceptions
- **Misconception:** All arteries carry oxygen-rich blood. **Correction:** Arteries carry blood away from the heart; pulmonary arteries carry oxygen-poor blood to the lungs.
- **Misconception:** The lungs pump blood. **Correction:** The heart pumps blood; the lungs exchange gases.

## Check Your Understanding

### Question 1
Which organ pumps blood?

**Answer:** The heart.
**Explanation:** The heart is a muscular pump.

### Question 2
Where does oxygen enter the blood?

**Answer:** At the alveoli in the lungs.
**Explanation:** Oxygen moves across the walls of tiny air sacs.

## Summary
- The lungs exchange oxygen and carbon dioxide.
- The heart and blood vessels transport gases and nutrients.
