# Removing Waste and Fighting Germs

## Learning Objectives
- Explain how kidneys help remove waste.
- Describe the immune system’s basic job.

## Why the Body Removes Waste

Cells produce waste as they work. The body uses several systems to remove wastes and keep its internal conditions stable.

## The Urinary System

The **kidneys** filter blood and help remove extra water and dissolved wastes as **urine**. Urine travels through the ureters to the **bladder**, where it is stored before leaving the body through the urethra.

## Other Ways Waste Leaves

The lungs release carbon dioxide when you breathe out. Skin releases sweat, which helps regulate temperature. The digestive tract removes undigested material as feces.

## The Immune System

The **immune system** helps protect the body from harmful germs. White blood cells and other defenses help recognize and fight many infections. Vaccines help train the immune system to recognize certain germs.

## Healthy Habits

Handwashing, recommended vaccinations, sleep, and covering coughs can help reduce the spread of illness.

## Key Vocabulary
- **kidney:** Organ that filters blood and helps make urine.
- **bladder:** Organ that stores urine.
- **germ:** An everyday word for a microorganism that may cause illness.
- **immune system:** The body’s defenses against many harmful invaders.

## Common Misconceptions
- **Misconception:** The bladder makes urine. **Correction:** The kidneys make urine; the bladder stores it.
- **Misconception:** All bacteria make people sick. **Correction:** Many bacteria are harmless or helpful.

## Check Your Understanding

### Question 1
Which organs filter blood to help form urine?

**Answer:** Kidneys.
**Explanation:** The kidneys remove wastes and regulate water balance.

### Question 2
What do white blood cells help do?

**Answer:** Fight infections.
**Explanation:** They are important parts of the immune system.

## Summary
- The kidneys help remove wastes through urine.
- The immune system helps protect against infections.
