# Nutrition, Energy, and Healthy Body Systems

## Learning Objectives
- Name major nutrient groups.
- Explain why food, water, activity, and sleep matter.

## Why We Need Food

Food supplies energy and materials for growth and repair. The body needs a variety of nutrients, including carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins, and minerals.

## What Different Nutrients Do

**Carbohydrates** are a major energy source. **Proteins** help build and repair body tissues. **Fats** provide stored energy and help the body absorb certain vitamins. **Vitamins and minerals** support many body processes.

## Water and the Body

Water helps move substances around the body, regulate temperature, and support chemical reactions. Drinking water regularly is important, especially during activity and hot weather.

## Activity and Sleep

Physical activity supports bones, muscles, and heart health. Sleep helps the body recover and supports learning and growth.

## Body Systems Together

After a meal, the digestive system absorbs nutrients. The circulatory system transports them. The respiratory system supplies oxygen that cells use to release energy from nutrients.

## Key Vocabulary
- **nutrient:** A substance in food the body needs to function.
- **protein:** A nutrient important for growth and tissue repair.
- **hydration:** Having enough water in the body.
- **vitamin:** An organic nutrient needed in small amounts for healthy function.

## Common Misconceptions
- **Misconception:** Only carbohydrates give the body energy. **Correction:** Carbohydrates, fats, and proteins can all provide energy.
- **Misconception:** You can stay healthy by eating only one kind of food. **Correction:** A varied diet helps supply different nutrients.

## Check Your Understanding

### Question 1
Which nutrient helps build and repair tissues?

**Answer:** Protein.
**Explanation:** Proteins help build and repair body structures.

### Question 2
Why is water important?

**Answer:** It supports transport and temperature control, among other jobs.
**Explanation:** Many processes in the body need water.

## Summary
- A balanced variety of nutrients supports different body systems.
- Water, activity, and sleep are also important for health.
