# Bones, Muscles, and Movement

## Learning Objectives
- Explain how bones support and protect the body.
- Describe how muscles and bones work together.

## What the Skeleton Does

The **skeletal system** provides structure, protects organs, and helps us move. The skull protects the brain, and the rib cage helps protect the heart and lungs. Many adults have **206 bones**; children generally have more bones that fuse as they grow.

## Muscles Make Movement Possible

Skeletal **muscles** pull on bones, usually through **tendons**. Muscles can contract (shorten) and relax, but they do not push bones. Muscle pairs often work together.

## Joints and Ligaments

A **joint** is a place where bones meet. **Ligaments** connect bone to bone and help stabilize joints. Tendons connect muscle to bone.

## Healthy Habits

Movement, nutritious food, sleep, and safety equipment help protect bones and muscles. Helmets and protective gear reduce injury risk during activities.

## Key Vocabulary
- **bone:** Hard living tissue that supports and protects the body.
- **joint:** The meeting place of two or more bones.
- **tendon:** Tissue that connects muscle to bone.
- **ligament:** Tissue that connects bone to bone.

## Common Misconceptions
- **Misconception:** Muscles push bones to move them. **Correction:** Skeletal muscles pull as they contract.
- **Misconception:** Bones are not alive. **Correction:** Bones are living tissue that can grow and repair.

## Check Your Understanding

### Question 1
What protects the brain?

**Answer:** The skull.
**Explanation:** The skull surrounds the brain.

### Question 2
Do tendons connect muscle to bone or bone to bone?

**Answer:** Muscle to bone.
**Explanation:** Ligaments connect bone to bone.

## Summary
- Bones support and protect; muscles help move the skeleton.
- Joints allow many kinds of movement.
