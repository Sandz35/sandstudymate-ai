# The Nervous System and Five Senses

## Learning Objectives
- Identify the brain, spinal cord, and nerves.
- Explain how senses help the body respond.

## The Body’s Communication Network

The **nervous system** includes the brain, spinal cord, and nerves. It receives information, processes it, and helps control the body’s responses.

## Brain and Spinal Cord

The **brain** helps us think, remember, feel, and coordinate actions. The **spinal cord** carries many messages between the brain and body and helps control some fast reflexes.

## The Five Traditional Senses

**Sight** uses the eyes; **hearing** uses the ears; **smell** uses the nose; **taste** uses taste buds (mainly on the tongue); **touch** uses receptors in the skin and other tissues. The body also senses balance, body position, and more.

## What Is a Reflex?

A **reflex** is a quick, automatic response, such as pulling your hand away from something hot. Some reflex circuits involve the spinal cord before the brain fully processes what happened.

## Protect Your Senses

Wear helmets when appropriate, keep sound volume at safe levels, and follow eye-safety rules during activities.

## Key Vocabulary
- **neuron:** A nerve cell that sends and receives signals.
- **spinal cord:** A bundle of nerve tissue connecting the brain to the body.
- **reflex:** A fast, involuntary response to a stimulus.
- **receptor:** A structure that detects a change or signal.

## Common Misconceptions
- **Misconception:** Humans have only five kinds of sensing. **Correction:** The five traditional senses are commonly taught, but balance and body position are also sensed.
- **Misconception:** Every movement must be consciously planned. **Correction:** Some responses happen automatically as reflexes.

## Check Your Understanding

### Question 1
What connects the brain with many nerves in the body?

**Answer:** Spinal cord.
**Explanation:** It carries messages between brain and body.

### Question 2
Which organ detects light for sight?

**Answer:** The eyes.
**Explanation:** Light-sensitive cells in the eyes send signals to the brain.

## Summary
- The nervous system carries information and coordinates responses.
- Sense organs help the body detect changes.
