# Cells, Tissues, Organs, and Systems

## Learning Objectives
- Describe how the human body is organized.
- Give examples of organs and systems.

## From Small to Large

Most of the human body is made of **cells**. Groups of similar cells doing related work form **tissues**. Different tissues combine to form **organs**, and organs work together in **organ systems**.

## Example: The Heart

Heart muscle cells are part of muscle tissue. The heart is an organ made from multiple kinds of tissue. It belongs to the circulatory system and pumps blood.

## Systems Work Together

The respiratory system brings oxygen into the body. The circulatory system transports oxygen to cells. The digestive system supplies nutrients. No system works completely alone.

## Real-Life Example

When you run, muscle cells need more oxygen and nutrients. Your breathing and heartbeat usually become faster to help supply them.

## Key Vocabulary
- **cell:** A basic unit of life.
- **tissue:** A group of cells performing related functions.
- **organ:** A structure made of different tissues that does a particular job.
- **organ system:** A group of organs working together.

## Common Misconceptions
- **Misconception:** The heart is a cell. **Correction:** The heart is an organ made of tissues.
- **Misconception:** Body systems never interact. **Correction:** Body systems support one another.

## Check Your Understanding

### Question 1
What is larger: a tissue or an organ?

**Answer:** An organ.
**Explanation:** Organs are built from multiple tissues.

### Question 2
Which system includes the heart?

**Answer:** The circulatory system.
**Explanation:** The heart moves blood throughout the body.

## Summary
- Cells form tissues, tissues form organs, and organs form systems.
- Body systems cooperate to keep us alive.
