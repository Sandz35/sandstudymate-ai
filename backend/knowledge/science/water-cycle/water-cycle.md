# The water cycle
## Evaporation
The Sun heats water in oceans, lakes and puddles. Some of it turns into an invisible
gas called water vapor and rises into the air.
## Condensation
As water vapor rises it cools and turns back into tiny liquid droplets, which gather
to form clouds. The drops on the outside of a cold glass are condensation too.
## Precipitation
When droplets in a cloud join together and get heavy, they fall as rain, snow, sleet
or hail.
## Collection
Water collects in oceans, lakes, rivers and underground. Then the cycle starts again.
The total amount of water on Earth stays about the same; it just keeps moving.
