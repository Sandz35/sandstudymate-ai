# Asteroids, Comets, and Meteors

## Learning Objectives
- Tell asteroids and comets apart.
- Distinguish meteoroid, meteor, and meteorite.
- Identify the asteroid belt.

## Asteroids

An **asteroid** is a small rocky or metallic object orbiting the Sun. Many asteroids are in the **asteroid belt** between Mars and Jupiter, though asteroids also exist elsewhere.

## Comets

A **comet** contains ice, dust, and rock. When a comet approaches the Sun, heat can produce a glowing cloud and tails of gas and dust. The tails often point generally away from the Sun.

## Meteoroid, Meteor, Meteorite

A **meteoroid** is a small rocky or metallic body traveling through space. A **meteor** is the bright streak seen when such material enters an atmosphere and heats up. A **meteorite** is a piece that survives and reaches the ground.

## Where Is the Kuiper Belt?

The **Kuiper Belt** is a region beyond Neptune with many icy objects, including Pluto.

## Real-Life Example

A "shooting star" is usually a **meteor**, not a star falling from the sky.

## Key Vocabulary
- **asteroid:** A small rocky or metallic object that orbits the Sun.
- **comet:** An icy object orbiting the Sun that can develop a cloud and tails.
- **meteoroid:** A small natural rocky or metallic object in space.
- **meteor:** A bright streak caused by material passing through an atmosphere.
- **meteorite:** Space material that reaches the ground.

## Common Misconceptions
- **Misconception:** A shooting star is an actual star. **Correction:** It is usually a meteor.
- **Misconception:** All asteroids orbit between Mars and Jupiter. **Correction:** Many do, but some follow other paths.

## Check Your Understanding

### Question 1
What do we call a space rock that reaches Earth’s ground?

**Answer:** Meteorite.
**Explanation:** The surviving piece is a meteorite.

### Question 2
What is a comet mainly known for?

**Answer:** Ices that can form a glowing cloud and tails near the Sun.
**Explanation:** Solar heating releases gas and dust.

## Summary
- Asteroids are commonly rocky; comets contain significant ice.
- Meteoroid, meteor, and meteorite refer to different stages or locations.
