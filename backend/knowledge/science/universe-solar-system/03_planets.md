# The Eight Planets

## Learning Objectives
- Name the eight planets in order.
- Compare rocky planets with giant planets.
- Give a distinctive fact about each planet.

## Planets in Order From the Sun

**Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune**. A memory sentence is: *My Very Educated Mother Just Served Us Noodles.*

## Inner Rocky Planets

**Mercury** is the closest planet to the Sun and the smallest planet. **Venus** is the hottest planet because its thick atmosphere traps heat. **Earth** has liquid water on its surface and is the only planet known to support life. **Mars** is called the Red Planet because iron-rich dust gives it a reddish color.

## Outer Giant Planets

**Jupiter** is the largest planet and has the Great Red Spot, a long-lasting storm. **Saturn** is famous for its large, bright ring system. **Uranus** rotates at an extreme tilt, almost on its side. **Neptune** is the farthest planet from the Sun and has extremely powerful winds.

## Rocky, Gas, and Ice Giants

Mercury, Venus, Earth, and Mars are **rocky planets**. Jupiter and Saturn are **gas giants**. Uranus and Neptune are often called **ice giants**, because their interiors differ from those of Jupiter and Saturn.

## What About Pluto?

**Pluto** is classified as a **dwarf planet**, not one of the eight planets. It orbits the Sun but has not cleared its orbital neighborhood of other objects.

## Key Vocabulary
- **planet:** A large, nearly round body orbiting a star; the eight solar-system planets have cleared their orbital neighborhoods.
- **rocky planet:** A planet with a solid rocky surface.
- **gas giant:** A giant planet made mostly of hydrogen and helium.
- **ice giant:** A giant planet with a different interior composition than gas giants.
- **dwarf planet:** A nearly round object orbiting the Sun that has not cleared its orbital neighborhood.

## Common Misconceptions
- **Misconception:** Mercury is the hottest because it is closest to the Sun. **Correction:** Venus is hotter because of its strong greenhouse effect.
- **Misconception:** Saturn is the only planet with rings. **Correction:** Jupiter, Uranus, and Neptune also have rings.

## Check Your Understanding

### Question 1
Which is the largest planet?

**Answer:** Jupiter.
**Explanation:** Jupiter is the largest of the eight planets.

### Question 2
Which planet is the hottest?

**Answer:** Venus.
**Explanation:** Its thick atmosphere traps heat.

### Question 3
Is Pluto one of the eight planets?

**Answer:** No.
**Explanation:** Pluto is classified as a dwarf planet.

## Summary
- There are eight recognized planets in our solar system.
- The first four are rocky; the outer four are giants.
