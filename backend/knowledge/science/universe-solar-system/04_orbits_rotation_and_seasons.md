# Orbits, Day and Night, and Seasons

## Learning Objectives
- Explain how Earth rotates and revolves.
- Describe why day and night happen.
- Describe the main cause of seasons.

## Rotation Makes Day and Night

Earth **rotates** (spins) on its axis about once every 24 hours. The side facing the Sun experiences daytime; the side facing away experiences nighttime.

## Revolution Makes a Year

Earth **revolves** around the Sun. One complete orbit takes about **365¼ days**, which is why we sometimes add a leap day to the calendar.

## Why We Have Seasons

Earth’s axis is tilted about **23.5 degrees**. As Earth orbits the Sun, the tilt changes how directly sunlight reaches different parts of Earth and how long daylight lasts. This is the **main cause of seasons**. Seasons are **not** caused mainly by Earth moving closer to or farther from the Sun.

## Northern and Southern Hemispheres

When the Northern Hemisphere tilts toward the Sun, it receives more direct sunlight and longer days: summer. At the same time, the Southern Hemisphere experiences winter.

## Gravity and Orbits

**Gravity** is the force that pulls objects toward one another. The Sun’s gravity helps keep the planets in orbit.

## Key Vocabulary
- **rotation:** Spinning around an axis.
- **revolution:** Traveling around another object.
- **orbit:** The path an object follows around another body.
- **axis:** An imaginary line about which an object rotates.
- **gravity:** A force of attraction between objects with mass.

## Common Misconceptions
- **Misconception:** The Sun travels around Earth once a day. **Correction:** Earth rotates, making the Sun appear to move across our sky.
- **Misconception:** Summer happens because Earth is much closer to the Sun. **Correction:** Seasons mainly result from Earth’s tilted axis.

## Check Your Understanding

### Question 1
What causes day and night?

**Answer:** Earth’s rotation.
**Explanation:** Different sides of Earth face the Sun as Earth spins.

### Question 2
What mainly causes seasons?

**Answer:** Earth’s axial tilt while orbiting the Sun.
**Explanation:** Tilt changes sunlight angle and day length.

## Summary
- Earth rotates roughly once a day and orbits the Sun roughly once a year.
- Earth’s tilt is the main cause of seasons.
