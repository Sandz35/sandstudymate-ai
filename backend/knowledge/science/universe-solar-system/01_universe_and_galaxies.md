# The Universe and Galaxies

## Learning Objectives
- Describe the universe and galaxies.
- Identify the Milky Way as our galaxy.
- Explain what a light-year measures.

## What Is the Universe?

The **universe** includes all space, time, matter, and energy. Stars, planets, galaxies, gas, and dust are parts of the universe. Scientists estimate that the universe began expanding about **13.8 billion years ago**.

## What Is a Galaxy?

A **galaxy** is a huge collection of stars, gas, dust, and other matter held together by gravity. Our Sun is one of the many stars in the **Milky Way galaxy**. Many galaxies exist beyond the Milky Way.

## Measuring Huge Distances

A **light-year** is the **distance** light travels in one year, not an amount of time. Light travels very fast—about 300,000 kilometers (186,000 miles) each second. Astronomers use light-years because space is so vast.

## Real-Life Connection

When you look at a star, its light may have traveled for years or much longer before reaching your eyes. Looking far into space means seeing light that started traveling long ago.

## Key Vocabulary
- **universe:** Everything in space, including matter and energy.
- **galaxy:** A vast group of stars and other material held together by gravity.
- **Milky Way:** The galaxy containing our solar system.
- **light-year:** The distance light travels in one year.

## Common Misconceptions
- **Misconception:** A light-year measures time. **Correction:** A light-year measures distance.
- **Misconception:** The Sun is the largest object in the universe. **Correction:** The Sun is one ordinary star among enormous numbers of stars.

## Check Your Understanding

### Question 1
Which galaxy contains Earth?

**Answer:** The Milky Way.
**Explanation:** Earth goes around the Sun, and the Sun is in the Milky Way.

### Question 2
Is a light-year a time or distance unit?

**Answer:** Distance.
**Explanation:** It tells how far light travels in one year.

## Summary
- Earth belongs to the solar system, which belongs to the Milky Way galaxy.
- Galaxies are only part of the much larger universe.
