# Electrical Energy and Power

## Electricity transfers energy

Electrical energy can be changed into other forms. Devices are useful because they turn electrical energy into light, sound, motion, or heat.

| Device | Main useful energy output |
|---|---|
| Lamp | Light |
| Fan | Motion of blades and air |
| Speaker | Sound |
| Toaster | Heat |
| Toy motor | Motion |

Some energy also spreads out in less useful forms, often heat. Energy is transferred or transformed, not created from nothing.

## Where does electrical energy come from?

- A **battery** stores chemical energy and changes it into electrical energy in a circuit.
- A **solar cell** changes energy from sunlight into electrical energy.
- A **generator** changes energy from motion into electrical energy, as in many power stations.

## What is power?

**Power** is how quickly energy is transferred or used. Power is measured in **watts (W)**. A 10-watt LED lamp uses electrical energy at a lower rate than a 60-watt incandescent lamp while each is on. A bulb's brightness also depends on its design, so watts are not a direct measure of brightness.

## Ways to save electrical energy

Turn off lights in empty rooms, unplug unused chargers when convenient, choose efficient LED bulbs, and use natural daylight where possible. Saving electrical energy can lower costs and reduce demand for power generation.

## Check your understanding

**Question:** What energy change happens when a fan runs?

**Answer:** The fan changes electrical energy mainly into motion, with some sound and heat too.
