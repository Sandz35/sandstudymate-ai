# Grade 5 Electricity Practice Questions

For a tutoring app, retrieve the question text without revealing the answer section until the learner responds.

## Q1 — Static charge

**Question:** What causes a balloon rubbed on hair to sometimes stick to a wall?

A. Static electric charge  
B. Gravity disappears  
C. The wall becomes a magnet  
D. The balloon produces sunlight

**Correct answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Rubbing can transfer electrons, leaving the balloon charged. Electric forces can attract it to the wall.  
**Hint:** Think about charge building up.

## Q2 — Complete circuit

**Question:** What must a simple battery-and-bulb circuit have for the bulb to light?

A. A broken wire  
B. A complete conducting loop  
C. Only one wire touching the battery  
D. A plastic strip in place of the wire

**Correct answer:** B  
**Explanation:** Electric current needs a complete path through the bulb and back to the battery.  
**Hint:** Think of a path with no gaps.

## Q3 — Insulators

**Question:** Which of these is usually an electrical insulator when dry?

A. Copper  
B. Aluminum  
C. Plastic  
D. Steel

**Correct answer:** C  
**Explanation:** Dry plastic strongly resists the flow of charge.  
**Hint:** Think about the covering around many electrical wires.

## Q4 — Open switch

**Question:** What happens to a simple circuit when its only switch opens?

A. Current flows faster  
B. The battery becomes a magnet  
C. The path breaks and the bulb turns off  
D. The wire turns into plastic

**Correct answer:** C  
**Explanation:** An open switch interrupts the complete loop.  
**Hint:** Can charge travel across a gap in a simple circuit?

## Q5 — Series circuit

**Question:** Two bulbs are in series. If removing one bulb breaks the circuit, what happens?

A. The other bulb keeps glowing  
B. Both bulbs go off  
C. The battery charges itself  
D. Both bulbs become magnets

**Correct answer:** B  
**Explanation:** A series circuit has only one path, so a break stops current everywhere along that path.  
**Hint:** Count the number of paths.

## Q6 — Parallel circuit

**Question:** Why can one bulb remain on when a bulb in another parallel branch is removed?

A. The branches give separate complete paths  
B. Parallel circuits never need batteries  
C. The other bulb creates electricity  
D. Plastic becomes a metal

**Correct answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Other branches can still form complete loops.  
**Hint:** Imagine two different routes.

## Q7 — Energy change

**Question:** A fan mostly changes electrical energy into which form?

A. Motion  
B. Chemical energy stored in food  
C. Light from the Sun  
D. Gravitational energy only

**Correct answer:** A  
**Explanation:** The motor spins the blades, making moving air.  
**Hint:** What do the blades do?

## Q8 — Power

**Question:** What does a device's power rating in watts tell us?

A. How quickly it uses or transfers energy  
B. Its color  
C. The number of batteries inside it  
D. How much it weighs

**Correct answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Power is the rate of energy transfer or use.  
**Hint:** Think about 'how quickly.'

## Q9 — Electromagnet

**Question:** What creates the magnetic field of an electromagnet?

A. An electric current  
B. A bright paint color  
C. Sunlight alone  
D. Plastic wrapping alone

**Correct answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Moving electric charge produces magnetic fields.  
**Hint:** Look at the word 'electro.'

## Q10 — Safety

**Question:** You notice a damaged electrical cord. What is the safest action?

A. Touch the bare wire to see if it works  
B. Put the cord in water  
C. Stop using it and tell an adult  
D. Wrap it around your hand

**Correct answer:** C  
**Explanation:** Damaged wires can cause shocks or fires, so an adult should handle the problem.  
**Hint:** Safety comes first.
