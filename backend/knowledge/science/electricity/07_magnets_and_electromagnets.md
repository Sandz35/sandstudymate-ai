# Electricity and Magnetism

## Moving charge creates a magnetic field

An electric current produces a magnetic field around a wire. A magnetic field is a region where magnetic forces can act.

## What is an electromagnet?

An **electromagnet** is a magnet made using electric current. A coil of wire carrying current creates a magnetic field. Wrapping a coil around an iron core can make the field stronger. Unlike an ordinary permanent magnet, an electromagnet can usually be turned on and off by controlling the current.

## Everyday uses

Electromagnets help power doorbells, speakers, electric motors, and machines that lift scrap metal.

## How can an electromagnet become stronger?

In a simple setup, adding more turns to the coil or increasing current can make the magnet stronger. However, too much current can overheat wires and batteries. Investigations should use only adult-supervised, current-limited low-voltage kits.

## Check your understanding

**Question:** Why can an electromagnet be switched off?

**Answer:** Its magnetic field comes from electric current. When current stops, the field produced by that current stops as well, though an iron core may keep a little magnetism.
