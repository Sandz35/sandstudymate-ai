# Percentages
## What a percent means
Percent means "out of 100". 25% means 25 out of 100, which is the same as the
fraction 25/100 = 1/4 and the decimal 0.25.
## Finding a percent of a number
To find 10% of a number, divide by 10: 10% of 80 is 8. To find 50%, divide by 2. To
find 25%, divide by 4. Build other percents from these: 30% of 80 is 3 x 8 = 24.
## Percents in everyday life
A 20% discount on a $50 toy saves 20% of 50 = $10, so the toy costs $40. A test score
of 18 out of 20 is 18/20 = 90/100 = 90%.
## Common mistakes
50% of a number is half of it, not the number minus 50. The same percent of a bigger
number is bigger: 10% of 200 is more than 10% of 20.
